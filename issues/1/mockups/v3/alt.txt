Swimlane diagram of the pj flow: pj-prep-the-repo runs once, then pj-grill-for-specs-readiness moves a Fresh issue to needs-input and stops while a human answers the questions, picks a mockup from pj-mock-it-up for a UI change, and signs off the spec, which makes the issue spec-ready. pj-implement (after pj-repro for a bug, or pj-decompose when the bite is too big) opens the PR, pj-review and pj-address-review loop on it until a human reviews and merges it to Done, and pj-next-round and pj-healthcheck are read-only at any time.
