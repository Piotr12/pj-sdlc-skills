pj flow: after pj-prep-the-repo, an issue goes from Fresh through pj-grill-for-specs-readiness (with needs-input until a human answers and signs off, and pj-mock-it-up for a UI change) to spec-ready, where pj-decompose can split it, then through pj-repro for a bug and pj-implement to Implemented, where pj-review and pj-address-review loop until a human merges it to Done. pj-next-round and pj-healthcheck are read-only.
