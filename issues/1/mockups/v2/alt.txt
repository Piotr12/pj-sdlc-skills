Issue flow, top to bottom: pj-prep-the-repo sets up the repo once; a Fresh issue goes through pj-grill-for-specs-readiness (with pj-mock-it-up for a UI change) to needs-input, where a human answers, signs off, and picks a mockup, and then to spec-ready; pj-repro, pj-implement, or pj-decompose take it to Implemented with a PR; pj-review and pj-address-review loop until a human merges, and the issue is Done. pj-next-round and pj-healthcheck are read-only and run any time.
