Flow of the pj skills, one card per skill: pj-prep-the-repo runs once per repo; pj-grill-for-specs-readiness moves a Fresh issue to needs-input until a human answers and signs off, then to spec-ready (pj-mock-it-up adds mockups for a UI change, and a human picks one); pj-implement moves a spec-ready issue to Implemented with a PR (pj-repro first writes a failing test for a bug, pj-decompose splits an issue that is too big into spec-ready children). pj-review and pj-address-review loop on the PR until a human reviews and merges it, and the issue is Done; pj-next-round and pj-healthcheck are read-only and run any time.
