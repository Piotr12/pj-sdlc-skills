pj issue flow: Fresh goes through pj-grill-for-specs-readiness to needs-input (a human answers and signs off) and to spec-ready, with pj-mock-it-up for UI changes; pj-repro (bugs) and pj-implement take spec-ready to Implemented with a PR, or pj-decompose splits a big issue; pj-review and pj-address-review loop on the PR until a human merges it and the issue is Done. Repo level: pj-prep-the-repo once, pj-healthcheck and pj-next-round read-only.
