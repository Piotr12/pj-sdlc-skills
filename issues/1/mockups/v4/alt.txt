Subway map of the pj flow: a Fresh issue goes through pj-grill-for-specs-readiness, which loops through needs-input for human answers and sign-off and through pj-mock-it-up for a human mockup pick, to spec-ready, then through pj-implement (bugs via pj-repro first, too-big issues back through pj-decompose to spec-ready) to Implemented, where pj-review and pj-address-review loop until a human merges and the issue is Done. pj-prep-the-repo prepares the repo once, and pj-next-round and pj-healthcheck are read-only.
